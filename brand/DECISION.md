# Brand decision: Output Ledger

Status: accepted by the brand strategist; the design evaluator judges it on the built product.

## Decision

Output Ledger: a quiet, ledger-like direction with one sharp signal red used only on the tracked number and actions.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Harbour chart**: Too geographic for an analytics tool; the subject is output metrics, not location.
- **Duty board**: Too operational for a prototype; the subject is tracking, not scheduling.
- **Glass dashboard**: A named cliché; glassmorphism panels on a blurred blob background are banned.

## Risks

- The ledger aesthetic may read as dated if not paired with a modern type system
- The signal red may feel aggressive if used beyond the tracked number and actions
- The offline-first positioning may confuse users expecting cloud sync

## Reversal

Revisit this decision when: If the product adds cloud sync or multi-user collaboration, the ledger direction should be revisited in favour of a shared-workspace direction.

Product: Output Pulse.
